# Media retention

Pricing and the owner plan card say that photos and other media are kept on a 12-month rolling basis, and that older media may be removed so storage stays within the plan. The sentence is the product copy. It is meant as ordinary housekeeping, not a warning that the account is unsafe.

Not implemented yet:

- a reminder email about six months before media would age out;
- a scheduled job that removes media older than 12 months.

Do not treat the copy as a live purge. Until that job exists, media stays until the owner deletes it or the account itself is closed under the existing closure process.
