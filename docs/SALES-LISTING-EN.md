# Klarraum — cleaning website and team job planning

Listing draft, not a published offer. Add the actual seller identity, price/tax terms and final license before publication.

A self-hosted German cleaning-company website with a price estimator, inquiry inbox, customer/property records, finite recurring job schedules, calendar, separate employee access and monthly management reports. One company per installation.

Includes source and lockfile; German/English installation manuals; owner branding/pricing/photo editor; server-validated inquiries; employee invitations; task timers and breaks; checklists and private proof photos; owner review; employee inbox; optional configured operational email; manual receipt/refund ledger; monthly snapshots and CSV/JSON/print-to-PDF; backup/restore tools.

Requirements: Node.js 24.13+, HTTPS and persistent writable storage. Optional mail uses a verified Resend sender. This is not a WordPress/PHP plugin. Interface controls offer German/English, with English buyer documentation. User/company content and legal documents are not automatically translated. Business reports keep Germany-specific time/currency conventions; unmapped validation messages may remain German.

Not included: hosting subscription, installation service unless separately ordered, online payments, payroll, certified statutory time tracking, GPS proof, customer signatures, native app, offline sync or shared multi-company SaaS. Reports are internal summaries, not invoices or a certified fiscal archive. Business/legal details and retention policies require company-specific setup.

Keep source licensing, installation/customization and ongoing support separate. State update term, response time, supported platforms and the backup operator. No promise of lifetime free support, unlimited capacity, guaranteed orders or existing revenue.

Use only fictional screenshots. Never publish owner credentials, API keys, invitation tokens or actual worker/customer information. The public free-hosted demonstration can lose test records on restart.
