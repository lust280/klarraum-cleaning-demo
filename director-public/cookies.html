Cookie Policy

The public registration pages do not set a cookie. After a director, company owner, or worker signs in, the browser stores one essential session cookie. It is used only to keep that sign-in and to check that later requests came from the same session. It is HTTP-only, ends when the session expires, and is not used for advertising.
